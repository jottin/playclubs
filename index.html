How to Play Clubs

Clubs is a trick-taking card game in which club cards decide the score. You play the Dealer (bottom of the screen) against three computer players. Everyone starts with 100 points, and the player with the most points after the last round wins. At the start of each round every player is issued a safe zone for how many clubs they will win, and one player goes without. End the round inside your zone to earn points; every club won outside it costs you 10.

1. Objective & scoring

Each round, try to finish inside the safe zone you were issued. Every club card is worth 10 points regardless of rank, and you collect the clubs that are in the books you win. Zones are scored when the round ends.

2. Setup & dealing

Every round starts with a fresh shuffle of a 52-card deck. Each of the four players gets 7 cards; the other 24 are set aside face-down and are not used that round, so the number of clubs in play varies. Aces are highest and 2s are lowest. A round has one book (trick) per card in a hand: 7 books.

3. Order of play

Play moves clockwise, starting with the round's leader. In round 1 the player to the Dealer's left (West) leads. Each later round the lead passes one seat clockwise: West, North, East, and then you, the Dealer, in round 4.

4. Safe zones

At the start of every round each player is issued one safe zone. Nobody bids or chooses: zones are handed out by seat and rotate one seat clockwise every round, so over four rounds every player holds each zone once and goes without a zone once. There are three zones, 0-2, 3-4 and 5-6 clubs, and one player has no zone. A player with no zone plays without a safety net: every club they win costs 10 points.

Zones are public. Every player's zone is shown on their scorecard next to the clubs they have won so far, and a target icon sits on the corner of each scorecard that holds a zone. Only clubs in the books you win count toward your total, not the number of books.

5. Gameplay

Each round is played in books. Starting with the leader and moving clockwise, every player plays one card to the book. The winner of a book collects it, so every club in it counts toward the winner's total. The winner then leads the next book.

  • Follow suit: you must match the leading suit if you can. If you cannot, you may play any card, including a club.
  • No clubs broken rule: clubs can lead a book at any time. Clubs can only be played off-suit when you cannot match the leading suit.

Winning books

The leading suit decides every book. Cards of any other suit, clubs included, can never win a book that a non-club led, however high they are.

  1. Nobody else matches the leading suit: the leader wins the book, whatever the rank. This applies to every suit.
  2. A club leads and is matched: the highest-ranked club wins.
  3. Any other suit leads and is matched: the highest-ranked card of the leading suit wins, even if one, two or three clubs were played.

6. Game options

The Settings button opens these options.

  • Book confirmation: on, a dialog appears after every book saying who won it and why. Off skips it.
  • Sound effects: turn the card sounds on or off.
  • Difficulty: Easy computer players sometimes make mistakes, Medium players play toward their own zone using the odds, and Hard players also look ahead.
  • Number of rounds: Easy plays 4 rounds, Medium 8 and Hard 12. Zones and leaders keep rotating the same way. A change to the number of rounds starts with the next game.
